# Privacy policy

Alex Language Explorer ("Alex") collects no personal data. Nothing you put into Alex leaves your device.

Effective 17 October 2026. This policy covers Alex on Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and Linux.

## What Alex stores

Alex keeps the following on your device, in its own app storage:

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Profile names and settings | To show each learner's rooms, phrases and look. |
| Voice recordings you make | To play phrases in a familiar voice. |

- Alex has no account, no sign-in and no server.
- Alex sends nothing over the network. The Android app does not request internet access.
- Alex has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Nothing is shared with anyone, including the developer.

## The microphone

- Alex uses the microphone only while an adult holds the record button.
- A recording is saved only when the adult presses Keep.
- You can turn off microphone access in your device's settings at any time. Alex keeps working, with no new recordings.

## Children

- Alex is made for children, and for the adults who set it up for them.
- Alex collects no data from children or from adults.
- Recordings stay on the device where they were made.

## Deleting your data

- To delete one profile and its recordings, choose **Edit** in the profile picker and hold the delete button.
- To delete everything, uninstall Alex.

## Other services

- Text-to-speech uses your device's own speech engine. Its provider's privacy policy covers it.
- Google Play and the App Store can collect crash and usage data from devices whose owners allow it. Google's
  and Apple's privacy policies cover that data. Alex adds nothing to it.

## Changes

Any change to this policy is published here, with a new effective date. Earlier versions are in this file's
history on GitHub.
