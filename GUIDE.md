# Caregiver guide

## Open caregiver mode

- On a phone or tablet, hold three fingers on the screen for three seconds.
- With a mouse, hold the left and right buttons together for three seconds.
- With a keyboard, hold Escape for three seconds.
- A bar fills while you hold. The picker opens when it is full.
- The bar starts over if your hand slides. Hold the hand in place and wait for it to fill.
- Nothing on the learner's screen leads here, so a tap or a swipe never leaves it.

## Starting the app

- The app opens on the profile used last.
- With no profile yet, the app opens on New profile.

## Profiles

- Choose a profile's name to open it for the learner.
- New profile asks for a name, a starting scene and a look.
- Edit changes a profile's look and its scenes.
- To delete a profile, choose Edit and hold the delete button for three seconds. Its recordings go with it,
  and there is no undo.

## Scenes

- A scene is a room to explore and the phrases that go with it.
- Add puts a scene's room and phrases in the profile.
- Remove takes a scene out. Phrases you recorded stay in the profile.
- Start here picks the room Explore opens on.

## Recording your voice

- Hold any object or phrase for two seconds to record its phrase.
- Hold the big button, say the phrase, and let go.
- Listen to the take, then press Keep. Nothing is saved before Keep.
- A new take replaces the old one. The old take is kept once, as a backup.
- Until a phrase has a recording, the device's voice says it.

## The learner's name

- Tap the name at the top to hear it.
- Hold the name for two seconds to record it.

## What the learner sees

- Explore is a room. Touching an object plays it and says its phrase.
- Words holds every phrase in the profile.
- Words has two ways to play. One is a column of phrases. The other builds a phrase from its parts.
- The view buttons look at the room from the front or from above. A drag moves the room and a pinch zooms.
- A moved view goes back to the front after two minutes with nothing touched.

## Privacy

- Nothing leaves the device. There is no account, no network and no analytics.
- Hold the privacy button in caregiver mode to read the full policy.
